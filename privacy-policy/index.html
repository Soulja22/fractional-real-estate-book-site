At a glance
We use your details to respond to your book enquiry, send the information you request and, where you choose, provide related property education updates. The embedded form also asks optional questions about property co-ownership. We do not ask for payment or bank details on this site.

This policy covers the Fractional Real Estate book website, its HighLevel/LeadConnector registration form and related communications.

Information we collect

  • Contact details you provide, including name, email address and telephone number.
  • Your answers to the form’s book and optional property education questions, including any stated interest in co-ownership and relevant capital range.
  • Communication preferences, consent and unsubscribe records.
  • Information from your interactions with our emails or website, and technical and campaign data such as device information, IP address, referral source, UTM parameters and click identifiers where available.
  • Information you send when you contact us.

Please do not enter payment-card details, bank information, identity documents or sensitive personal information in the form.

How and why we use your information

Book enquiries

We use your details to acknowledge your registration and provide information about the book’s release and availability. Our lawful basis is our legitimate interest in responding to an enquiry you initiated and, where applicable, steps taken at your request.

Optional property education

If you select related updates, we use your preferences and responses to send relevant educational information about property ownership and co-ownership. We rely on your consent for optional marketing and you can withdraw it at any time.

Operations and compliance

We also use information to secure the site and form, maintain consent and suppression records, handle requests and meet legal obligations. We rely on applicable legal obligations and legitimate interests in operating and protecting our services.

The form’s answers may help route follow-up information. They do not by themselves make an investment decision, create a contract, reserve property or determine a legal right. You can ask us to review a routing outcome by contacting us.

Sharing and international transfers

HighLevel/LeadConnector hosts the embedded form and CRM. We may also use website hosting, security and email providers working on our instructions, and share information with professional advisers or authorities when required.

Some providers may process information outside the United Kingdom, including in the United States. Authorised personnel may access information from Nigeria. Where a restricted transfer occurs, we use an available UK transfer mechanism and appropriate safeguards. Contact us for details relevant to your information.

How long we keep information

We normally keep book enquiry and related response information for no longer than 24 months after your last meaningful interaction, unless we need to keep it longer for legal obligations or claims. Consent, unsubscribe and suppression records may be retained longer where necessary to respect your choices and demonstrate compliance.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may ask to access, correct or delete your information; restrict or object to processing; receive certain information in a portable format; or withdraw consent. You have an absolute right to object to direct marketing. Use an unsubscribe link in an email or contact admin@propertywealthcorp.com.

Advertising and tracking

Links to this site may include campaign parameters or Google click identifiers. The embedded HighLevel form may use browser storage needed to provide and secure it. This site does not currently place Google Analytics, Meta Pixel or Google Ads conversion tags. If non-essential analytics or advertising storage is added later, this notice and any required consent controls should be updated before activation.

Security, children and changes

We use reasonable organisational and technical measures to protect information. No online service can guarantee absolute security. This site is intended for adults aged 18 or over. We may update this policy when our practices or legal obligations change; the latest date will appear above.

Contact and complaints

For privacy questions or requests, contact Property Wealth Corporation Limited at admin@propertywealthcorp.com or Suite 2072 Letraset Building, Wotton Road, Ashford, England, TN23 6LN.

You may complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or 0303 123 1113.

This policy describes the book site as built. Please review it if the form fields, CRM workflows, analytics, advertising tags or hosting arrangements change.
